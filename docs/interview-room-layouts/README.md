# Interview room layouts

Screenshots of the built-in interview room with 1 to 4 people, and with a screen share, on three screen sizes. They were taken from the interviewer's side in the local app on 11 November 2026, with people joining one at a time. Every camera is a browser test pattern, which is why all the pictures are green.

| Size | Viewport |
|---|---|
| Desktop | 1440 × 900 |
| Tablet, upright | 834 × 1112 |
| Phone | 390 × 844 |

## At a glance

| Who is in the room | Layout | Desktop | Tablet | Phone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [1 · Interviewer alone](#1-person) | Your own camera fills the frame | Good | Good | Good |
| [2 · + candidate](#2-people) | Candidate large, you in the corner | Good | Good | Corner tile is large |
| [3 · + one of the hiring team](#3-people) | Interviewer and candidate side by side, the third person below | Good | Cameras cropped | Cameras cropped |
| [4 · + two of the hiring team](#4-people) | Interviewer and candidate side by side, two smaller tiles below | Good | Cropped, labels wrap | Cropped, labels wrap |

## 4 people, screen share with Chat, Participants or Notes open

While someone shares their screen, opening Chat, Participants or Notes moves the cameras (you, the candidate and the hiring team) into a row under the shared screen, so the screen stays large next to the panel. Closing the panel puts the cameras back in the column on the right. This works the same in the interviewer's, the candidate's and a guest's room.

| Panel | Desktop | Tablet |
|---|---|---|
| Chat | ![Chat open while sharing, desktop](panel-chat-sharing-desktop.png) | ![Chat open while sharing, tablet](panel-chat-sharing-tablet.png) |
| Participants | ![Participants open while sharing, desktop](panel-participants-sharing-desktop.png) | ![Participants open while sharing, tablet](panel-participants-sharing-tablet.png) |
| Notes | ![Notes open while sharing, desktop](panel-notes-sharing-desktop.png) | ![Notes open while sharing, tablet](panel-notes-sharing-tablet.png) |

- **Tablet:** the panel floats over the room, so the screen and the camera row move to the left of it. The row is narrow there and scrolls sideways to reach the last camera.
- **Phone:** unchanged. The cameras are already in a row under the screen, and the panel covers the room while it is open.
