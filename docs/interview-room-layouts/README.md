# Interview room layouts

Screenshots of the built-in interview room with 1 to 4 people, and with a screen share, on three screen sizes. They were taken from the interviewer's side in the local app, with people joining one at a time. Desktop and tablet were taken on 11 November 2026; the phone pictures were retaken on 14 November 2026 after the phone layout was fixed. Every camera is a browser test pattern, which is why all the pictures are green.

| Tablet, upright | 834 × 1112 |

## 4 people, screen share with Chat, Participants or Notes open

While someone shares their screen, opening Chat, Participants or Notes moves the cameras (you, the candidate and the hiring team) into a row under the shared screen, so the screen stays large next to the panel. Closing the panel puts the cameras back in the column on the right. This works the same in the interviewer's, the candidate's and a guest's room.

| Panel | Desktop | Tablet | Phone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chat | ![Chat open while sharing, desktop](panel-chat-sharing-desktop.png) | ![Chat open while sharing, tablet](panel-chat-sharing-tablet.png) | ![Chat open while sharing, phone](panel-chat-sharing-phone.png) |
| Participants | ![Participants open while sharing, desktop](panel-participants-sharing-desktop.png) | ![Participants open while sharing, tablet](panel-participants-sharing-tablet.png) | ![Participants open while sharing, phone](panel-participants-sharing-phone.png) |
| Notes | ![Notes open while sharing, desktop](panel-notes-sharing-desktop.png) | ![Notes open while sharing, tablet](panel-notes-sharing-tablet.png) | ![Notes open while sharing, phone](panel-notes-sharing-phone.png) |

- **Tablet:** the panel floats over the room, so the screen and the camera row move to the left of it. The row is narrow there and scrolls sideways to reach the last camera.
- **Phone:** the panel is a full-screen sheet over the room, with the top bar and the controls still showing. The ✕ (40 × 40 px) closes it and the call is back. A phone has no room beside the panel, so a bottom drawer that leaves the video visible is a possible next step. The interviewer's Notes panel is closed when the room opens on a phone (on a larger screen it opens with the room); Notes is one tap away and nothing typed is lost.

## Small phones and phones on their side

| Small phone, 360 × 640 | Phone on its side, 844 × 390 |
|---|---|
| ![4 people, small phone](4-people-phone-small.png) | ![4 people, phone on its side](4-people-phone-landscape.png) |

- **Notch and home bar:** the top bar and the control row add the device's safe-area insets, so nothing sits under a notch, the rounded corners or the home bar. These pictures were taken without insets; with them the bars grow by the inset and the video area shrinks to fit.
- **Eight controls at 360 px:** the gaps shrink so the whole row, including the End button, fits without scrolling.
- **On its side:** the bars slim down (48 px top, 64 px controls, no labels) so the video keeps the height. With a screen share the camera grid moves to the right of the screen instead of a row under it that left the screen about 140 px tall.
- **Tap targets:** every call control, the panel's ✕ and tabs, Admit / Deny and Keep waiting, and the spotlight buttons are at least 40 px tall on a phone.
- **Before the call:** the device check no longer runs wider than the phone, and its join button stays at the bottom of the screen while the page scrolls.
